Today's Topic: A Digital Transformation Design for Cats Eye

Current State & Vision

  • Current State: A heritage brand (since 1980) with a basic online presence.
  • The Challenge: E-commerce sales are modest compared to offline retail.
  • The Vision: Become a digital-first lifestyle brand, seamlessly integrating online and offline channels.

Customer-Centric Strategy

  • Market Segmentation: Identify and target core segments.
  • Personalized Experiences: Use customer data for tailored offers.
  • Omnichannel Consistency: Ensure a seamless brand experience.

Technology Infrastructure

  • E-commerce Website & App: A fast, secure, mobile-first platform.
  • Local Payment Integration: Offer bKash, Nagad, etc.
  • Inventory Management (ERP): Real-time stock synchronization.
  • CRM: A central hub for all customer data.

Digital Marketing Approach

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO): Improve Google search ranking.
  • Paid Advertising: Run targeted campaigns on social media and Google.

Digital Marketing Approach

  • Social Media & Influencers: Engage community and collaborate with influencers.
  • Content Marketing: Create valuable content like styling guides.

Omni-channel Integration

  • Unified Fulfillment: Enable "Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store" (BOPIS).
  • In-Store Digital Experiences: Use QR codes and interactive kiosks.
  • Cross-Channel Promotions: Run unified campaigns and loyalty programs.

Supply Chain & Operations

  • Automation: Use barcoding/RFID for efficient inventory tracking.
  • Logistics Partnerships: Partner with 3rd-party delivery services.
  • Supplier Integration: Digitize communication with suppliers.

Employee Training & Change Management

  • Training Programs: Train staff on all new digital tools.
  • Digital Culture: Foster a "digital-first" mindset.
  • Upskilling & Hiring: Hire digital experts and upskill existing staff.

Customer Engagement & Loyalty

  • Loyalty Programs: Reward repeat customers with points and benefits.
  • Personalized Communication: Use CRM for targeted marketing.
  • Excellent Support: Offer quick support via live chat or WhatsApp.

Data Analytics & KPIs

Measure what matters to make data-driven decisions:

  • Conversion Rate: Visitors who become buyers.
  • Average Order Value (AOV): Average spend per order.
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV): Total revenue from a customer.
  • Return on Investment (ROI): Profitability of campaigns.

Innovation & Scaling

  • Emerging Tech: Pilot new technologies like AI chatbots or AR try-on tools.
  • New Markets: Explore selling to the Bangladeshi diaspora and regional exports.
  • Product Expansion: Use sales data to introduce new product lines like shoes or accessories.
  • Sustainability: Highlight eco-friendly and ethical practices to attract conscious consumers.
